Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is KRAYVO?

KRAYVO is a multi‑marketplace discovery platform that lets you browse and compare products from KRAYVO Originals, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho, Savana, and more – all in one place.

2. How do I purchase a product?

For KRAYVO Originals, you can buy directly on our site using secure Razorpay payment. For products from other platforms, clicking "Buy Now" will redirect you to the official store where you can complete your purchase safely.

3. Is KRAYVO free to use?

Absolutely! Browsing and discovering products on KRAYVO is completely free. We may earn a small commission from some partner stores at no extra cost to you.

4. Do I need an account to shop?

No account is required to browse products. However, creating an account lets you save your wishlist, track orders (for KRAYVO products), and get personalised recommendations.

5. Are prices real‑time?

We update prices as frequently as possible. For KRAYVO Originals, prices are live. For third‑party platforms, the final price may vary slightly at the time of purchase on their website.

6. How can I contact support?

Visit our Contact page for email and other details. We're here to help!
