About KRAYVO

KRAYVO is not just another shopping website. We are a premium product discovery platform that unites the biggest online marketplaces under one roof. Whether you're looking for the latest fashion on Myntra, top electronics on Amazon, or exclusive deals on KRAYVO Originals – we bring it all to you in a seamless, beautiful experience.

Our Vision

We believe online shopping should be effortless, inspiring, and trustworthy. KRAYVO was built to eliminate the need to jump between multiple apps and websites. We aggregate real‑time product listings, compare prices, and present them with a clean, ad‑free interface. No clutter, no distractions – just the best deals across India's favourite stores.

What Makes Us Different?

  • 🌐 Multi‑Platform Integration: KRAYVO, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho, Savana – all accessible from one search bar.
  • ⚡ Live Prices & Instant Updates: Our backend syncs with the latest product data, so you never miss a price drop.
  • 💎 Premium Experience: Glassmorphism design, smooth animations, dark/light mode, and a native‑app feeling.
  • 🔒 Secure Checkout: KRAYVO Originals purchases are processed via Razorpay; third‑party products redirect you to official stores for safe transactions.
  • ❤️ Community First: Wishlist, compare, and share products. We're building a community of smart shoppers.
